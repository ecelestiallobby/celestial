THE 3S STANDARDS & COMMITMENT

At Celestial Lobby, protecting your privacy, trust, and experience is the core foundation of our operations. We operate under three non-negotiable principles—Security, Secrecy, and Safety—to ensure every interaction and delivery is seamless, low-profile, and fully protected.

1. SECURITY (System & Identity Protection)

• Client Confidentiality: All interactions are tied strictly to unique identification codes, ensuring personal details remain isolated from logs.
• Encrypted Communication: End-to-end encrypted messaging infrastructure built directly into our platform.
• Access Control: System privileges are compartmentalized to prevent unauthorized exposure.

2. SECRECY (Complete Discretion & Privacy)

• Zero-Footprint Policy: Operational intelligence and rosters are handled on a strict need-to-know basis.
• Metadata Protection: Transmissions and receipts are sanitized to remove tracking tags and location markers.
• Automated Data Retention: Transaction details adhere to automated cleanup routines post-confirmation.

3. SAFETY (Fulfillment & Handling Integrity)

• Verification Standards: Double-verification checks for structural integrity and precise weight/specs.
• Anonymous Packaging: Unbranded, tamper-evident packaging designed for discreet transit.
• Verified Delivery: Two-point handoff procedures ensuring secure arrival without external footprints.

🌌 The Celestial Lobby Non-Negotiables (The 15Ns)

To maintain absolute operational integrity, discretion, and the highest standard of safety across our ecosystem, Celestial Lobby strictly enforces our non-negotiable store policies:

🛑 The Core 5Ns (Store & Operational Policies)

1. No COD (Cash on Delivery) ❗️

• Policy: Advance payment verification is mandatory before any order is confirmed or processed.

2. No Meet Ups ❗️

• Policy: All fulfillment operates through designated secure channels (such as ride-hailing app delivery or unattended drop-off protocols) to ensure absolute security and privacy.

3. No Refunds ❗️

• Policy: Confirmed orders are non-refundable under standard circumstances, with strict product-equivalent or non-cash resolution policies governing exceptional high-value reviews.

4. No Rush Orders ❗️

• Policy: Standard preparation and verification workflows must be respected. Rushing compromises our rigorous handling integrity.

5. No Spamming ❗️

• Policy: Unauthorized messaging, repeated policy infractions, or out-of-channel disruptions result in immediate account termination.

🛡️ The Behavioral 5Ns (Client Conduct & Boundaries)

6. No Rudeness ❗️

• Policy: Interactions with our support team and couriers must remain professional and civil. Disrespectful, abusive, or hostile language results in immediate termination of service and a permanent block.

7. No Nosy Probes ❗️

• Policy: Inquiries into confidential internal operations—including supplier identities, sourcing locations, wholesale acquisition costs, or profit margins—are strictly prohibited.

8. No Nuisance ❗️

• Policy: Creating unnecessary operational gridlock, frivolous disputes, or wasting administrative time with unverified claims will lead to blacklisting.

9. No Threats ❗️

• Policy: Any form of intimidation, coercion, blackmail, or legal/physical threats directed at staff, automated channels, or couriers triggers an immediate permanent ban and forfeiture of active orders.

10. No Bogus Transactions ❗️

• Policy: Submitting fake proof-of-payment receipts, false identity details, or intentionally misleading information results in an instant ban across all channels.

🔒 The Expanded 5Ns (Security & Operational Safeguards)

11. No Unverified Handling Fee Absorption ❗️

• Policy: Any unverified or extra fees incurred due to client errors in routing or payment details must be settled independently; internal absorption of unverified fees is strictly not permitted.

12. No Manual Address Telemetry Mismatches ❗️

• Policy: Submitted GPS map coordinates must strictly match your manual delivery address text. Discrepancies will trigger automated delivery holds.

13. No Unapproved Routing Shifts ❗️

• Policy: Once dispatch authorization is granted, changing drop-off locations mid-transit is prohibited to maintain courier discretion and safety protocols.

14. No Unauthorized Sharing of Access Codes ❗️

• Policy: Distributing client access gateways, unique 8-digit client codes, or invitation links outside authorized channels results in an immediate, permanent ban.

15. No Promotional Code Abuse on First Orders ❗️

• Policy: First-time purchasers are strictly prohibited from using promotional codes or special access perks on their initial transaction to preserve integrity verification.
